IMPORTANT BIRD AREAS & WETLANDS

Tanbi Wetland Complex

Extensive mangrove and estuarine habitats; an important stopping and feeding area for Palearctic migrants.

Tanji Bird Reserve & Bijol Islands

Coastal roosting and feeding habitat for gulls, terns, waders and pelicans.

Bao Bolong Wetland Reserve

Riverine and freshwater wetland habitats supporting diverse waterbirds.

Kotu Creek & Abuko

Accessible wetland and woodland habitats near the Greater Banjul Area.

Kiang West & River Gambia

Important inland mosaics of wetlands, mangroves and savanna habitats.

METHODOLOGY & DATA

The map combines reference locations with generalized seasonal flyway paths. Species are examples of migratory birds recorded in or associated with The Gambia. Replace schematic paths with vetted GPS tracks or occurrence records for quantitative analysis.
