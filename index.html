Project description

This application presents a spatial overview of selected migratory bird species associated with West African wintering areas, including wetlands and coastal habitats in The Gambia.

The map connects broad geographical stages of migration from northern and western Europe through the Iberian Peninsula, North Africa and the western Sahel towards The Gambia.

Gambian habitat locations include coastal reserves, estuaries, mangrove wetlands, freshwater environments and protected areas.

Methodology

1. Species selection: Five selected species are represented using published information on their broad distribution and migration ecology.

2. Corridor representation: Intermediate geographical points are connected to form schematic migration corridors between broad source regions and West African destinations.

3. Habitat mapping: Selected Gambian wetlands, protected areas, coastal habitats and birding locations are displayed as geographic reference points.

4. Seasonal interpretation: The monthly control illustrates broad seasonal phases rather than measured migration observations.

5. Visualisation: Leaflet is used for interactive mapping, route display, waypoint markers, habitat selection and basemap switching.

Data limitations

Important scientific qualification

The routes and intermediate points displayed in this application are generalised illustrative corridors. They are not GPS telemetry observations, verified individual bird tracks, confirmed stopover records, or measured migration paths.

The map does not currently load individual tracking records from Movebank or another telemetry database. Habitat markers are geographic reference locations and do not imply that every selected species occurs at every site.

The application should therefore be used for research communication, education and spatial exploration, not as a substitute for field observations or species-specific tracking data.
